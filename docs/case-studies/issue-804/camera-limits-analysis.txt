CAMERA LIMITS ANALYSIS
======================

Issue: Player spawns at bottom of Docks map where camera cannot reach

TECHNICAL FINDINGS:
------------------

1. Docks Map Dimensions (from DocksLevel.tscn):
   - Width: ~5128 pixels (0 to 5128)
   - Height: ~4128 pixels (0 to 4128)
   - Navigation area: 64 to 5064 (width), 64 to 4064 (height)

2. Player Spawn Position (line 892 in DocksLevel.tscn):
   - Position: Vector2(200, 3900)
   - This is near the bottom of the map

3. Camera Limits (from scenes/characters/csharp/Player.tscn, lines 68-72):
   - limit_left = 0
   - limit_top = 0
   - limit_right = 4128
   - limit_bottom = 3088

4. The Problem:
   - Player spawns at Y = 3900
   - Camera can only reach Y = 3088
   - Gap: 3900 - 3088 = 812 pixels
   - Player spawns 812 pixels BELOW the camera's maximum view range!
   - This makes the player invisible at spawn

5. Additional Issues:
   - Camera limit_right = 4128, but map extends to 5128 (1000 pixels short)
   - Camera limit_bottom = 3088, but map extends to 4128 (1040 pixels short)

ROOT CAUSE:
-----------
The camera limits in Player.tscn are hardcoded for smaller maps (approximately 4128x3088).
The Docks map is much larger (5128x4128), but the camera wasn't updated to support it.

SOLUTION:
---------
Update camera limits in Player.tscn to accommodate the full Docks map:
- limit_right should be at least 5128
- limit_bottom should be at least 4128

This will allow the camera to follow the player across the entire Docks map.
